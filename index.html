Employee handbook — excerpts

Welcome to Frenchie's

Welcome to the family. Frenchie's is a working restaurant at 8 Elgin Ave in Legion Square. You are expected to show up in uniform, on time, and ready to look after the guests. Treat the staff beside you the way you would treat a paying customer. Anything you cannot solve on the floor, you bring to a Shift Lead. Anything the Shift Lead cannot solve, they bring to Operations or to me.

Chain of command

Owner → Operations Manager and HR Director → Shift Lead → Cook, Waiter, Hostess → New Hire. Go one rung at a time. Skipping the ladder without cause is itself a conduct issue.

  • Samael Hesperus — Owner — 5869124
  • Kenzie Long — HR Director — 636
  • Leon Wagner — Operations Manager — 3956037

Probation

Every new hire serves a seven-day probation. During probation you are shadowed, trained on a station, and reviewed by HR at the end of the week. Pass and you move onto the ladder proper. Fall short and we part ways without prejudice.

Three-strike discipline

  • Strike one — verbal warning, noted in your file.
  • Strike two — written warning with a follow-up review date.
  • Strike three — termination.

Suspension may be issued at any point while a matter is being looked into. Theft from the register, violence toward guests or staff, and showing up to a shift unfit to work are immediate escalations.

Radio codes

  • 10-1 — clocking in
  • 10-2 — clocking out
  • 10-4 — understood / acknowledged
  • 10-20 — your current location
  • 10-99 — emergency, all hands

Bar register

The bar register runs a flat $1,000 rate per the handbook. That figure is the counter price the guest pays — it is not a wage, a payroll rate, or anything that touches your pay stub. Wages are hours × your role rate, paid Sunday 9:00 AM from the house.

Payday

Payroll runs Sunday at 9:00 AM. Payments go out from the house. Hours are logged by HR through the week — if your hours look wrong, raise it before Sunday, not after.
